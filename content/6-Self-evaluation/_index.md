### Strengths

- **Steady learning:** I went through a different AWS topic each week and recorded what I did in the worklog, from IAM and networking to monitoring, CI/CD and encryption keys.
- **Security mindset:** Because my major is Information Assurance, I pay attention to least privilege, secret management and cleaning up resources after each lab.
- **Learning by doing:** I prefer to build things myself, read the official documentation and look for the cause of an error before asking for help.

### Weaknesses

- **Limited depth:** I understand the main services at a basic level, but some of them I have only used in small labs.
- **Speed with new concepts:** Some topics, such as envelope encryption, took me longer to understand than I expected.
- **Communication:** I am still not confident when presenting technical ideas, and sometimes I keep a question to myself for too long before asking.

### Areas for improvement

- Go deeper into AWS security services instead of only knowing the basics of many services.
- Practise explaining technical topics through blog posts and short presentations.
- Ask for feedback earlier and share my ideas more often when working in a team.
