### Overall impression

Before the program, I only knew AWS through documentation and a few tutorial videos. After 12 weeks, the clearest change is that I am now used to building things myself, breaking them, and fixing them. It is slower than reading theory, but it stays with me much longer.

### What I appreciated

- **Mentors and the support team:** they did not hand me the answers but pointed me in a direction to find them. It was hard at first, but it taught me to read the official documentation and debug on my own.
- **A clear roadmap:** the content moved from core services to the final workshop, so each week I knew where I was and what came next.
- **Keeping a worklog:** I first saw them as a formality, then realized they showed me whether I truly understood something or had only followed the steps.
- **Events:** hearing from people who work with the cloud every day helped me see how real work differs from a lab.

### Learning environment and people

- **Friendly and supportive people:** the mentors and the program team are friendly, approachable and positive. They made me feel comfortable asking questions, even very basic ones.
- **A positive environment:** the atmosphere is open and encourages everyone to learn, share and try new things without being afraid of making mistakes.
- **Professional but comfortable:** I could see a professional way of working while still feeling welcome as a student.
- **Good peers:** the other interns are friendly and willing to help, which made learning and working together more enjoyable.

### Satisfaction level

**10/10.** I am very satisfied with this internship, both with what I learned and with the people I met. The points below are only suggestions to make the program even better.

### What I hope can be improved

- Some labs no longer match the current console interface, so newcomers lose time working out the differences. I hope the materials can be updated more often.
- I would like a mid-term review session to get early feedback on the workshop direction, instead of leaving it to the final weeks.
- Cost management should be stressed from the first week, since beginners easily forget to delete resources after a lab.

### Would I recommend the program?

Yes. If a friend asked me whether to join, I would say yes, because they can learn by doing, meet people who work with the cloud and be part of a supportive environment, as long as they are ready to learn on their own and not afraid to ask.

### A final note

Thank you to the mentors and the organizing team for your enthusiasm and patience with my questions.
